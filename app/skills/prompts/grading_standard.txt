Grade H (Extremely high-value lead): High probability of conversion if proactively contacted. Meets at least one of:
- Explicit purchase signal: directly expresses intent to buy, trade in, test drive, or visit a dealership
  (e.g. "I want to buy one", "thinking about switching cars", "want to go test drive it", "going to check it out at the store")
- Deep and sustained car-buying interest: no explicit purchase mention, but comments across multiple posts
  or over time show serious engagement -- repeatedly discussing specific personal use-case needs,
  comparing models with conclusions, tracking a model's developments over time.
  NOTE: a single in-depth comment without sustained multi-comment evidence does NOT qualify for H.
- Clear preference for our brand/models: explicitly states our model is superior to competitors,
  AND has some purchase-intent background visible in other comments.

Grade A (High-value lead): Good probability of conversion. Meets at least one of:
- Identifiable transaction or action signals: asking about price (total cost, final landed price),
  financing options (subsidies, installment, promotions, trade-in), dealer inventory or delivery timeline
- Single but substantive purchase intent: clearly comparing competing models on pros/cons,
  deeply discussing configuration differences, focusing on after-sales policy or cost of ownership;
  intent is clear but continuity across multiple comments is absent.
KEY DISTINCTION from H: A single substantive signal = A; multiple comments over time mutually reinforcing = H.

Grade B (Moderate-value lead): Lower priority than H/A. Meets at least one of:
- Some product interest: discussing exterior/interior, basic specs, "looks nice", generic positive attention
- Weak intent expression: vague or surface-level; purchase leaning is present but not strong enough
  to confidently determine buying intent

Grade C (Low-value lead): Purchase intent is minimal or absent.
Ordinary complaints, memes, surface-level emotional expressions, or bystander comments.

General principles:
- Evaluate based on the user's ENTIRE comment history, never let one comment dominate the verdict.
- Multiple similar comments that positively reinforce purchase intent count as cumulative evidence
  and may warrant upgrading the grade (e.g. repeated mentions of wanting to switch cars across
  multiple videos can push B to A, or A to H).
- When multiple grades apply simultaneously, take the highest.
- Fields without evidence remain unknown (null or empty array); do not infer occupation, income,
  or family situation.
